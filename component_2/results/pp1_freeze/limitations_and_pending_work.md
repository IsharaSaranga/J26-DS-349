# PP1 Research Limitations and Pending Work

- Real Component 1 aspect-sentiment outputs have not yet been integrated.
- Synthetic C1 fixtures are integration-test inputs, not validated customer minority-opinion labels.
- Some Function 1 usefulness scores used for selector evaluation are in-sample.
- Smartphone and laptop Function 1 models use different feature configurations.
- The evaluation uses controlled ten-product pilots rather than representative category populations.
- Prototype fidelity and minority improvements do not establish statistical generalization.
- The audit checks saved artifacts and consistency. It does not retrain models or independently reproduce the original data extraction.

## Post-PP1 Work

- Integrate actual C1 outputs.
- Implement appropriately held-out or out-of-fold usefulness scoring for selector evaluation.
- Extend datasets and product-level evaluation.
- Conduct controlled cross-category experiments.
- Complete final statistical and robustness validation.
